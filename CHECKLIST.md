# 5-minute phone checklist

Do this parked, on the iPhone, in **Safari** (not a Home Screen icon). Tick each box; if one fails, note what you saw.

**Before you start:** open your site address in Safari. Close the "Using Car Radio" pop-up with **Got it**.

## 1. Two taps to audio (1 min)
- [ ] Tap **News**, then **World**. BBC World Service plays within about 5 seconds.
- [ ] Tap the big **next** arrow. A different World station plays. Tap **previous** to go back.
- [ ] Tap **back** twice to Home. Tap **Sports**, then **Soccer**. talkSPORT plays.

## 2. Screen locked, Safari tab (1 min)
- [ ] With a station playing, press the side button to lock the phone. Audio keeps playing.
- [ ] On the lock screen you see the station name, play/pause, and **next/previous** (not "15 seconds" buttons).
- [ ] Tap **next** on the lock screen. The station changes and keeps playing.
- [ ] Tap **pause**, wait 10 seconds, tap **play**. It picks up the live broadcast again.

## 3. Wi-Fi to cellular (1 min)
- [ ] Start a station on Wi-Fi. Open Control Center and turn **Wi-Fi off**.
- [ ] Within about 10–15 seconds the same station is playing again over cellular. A short gap is normal.
- [ ] Turn Wi-Fi back on. Playback continues, or recovers within about 15 seconds.

## 4. A dead stream skips by itself (1 min)
- [ ] In Safari, add `?test=dead` to the end of your site address (e.g. `https://YOUR-NAME.github.io/car-radio/?test=dead`) and open it.
- [ ] Tap **News → World**. Within a few seconds you see "Skipped Test dead station, it didn't play", and BBC World Service plays without a tap.
- [ ] Open the normal address again (without `?test=dead`) when done.
- [ ] Optional: turn on **Airplane Mode** while playing. You see "No connection. Will resume when it comes back." Turn it off; playback returns within about 15 seconds.

## 5. A favorite survives a reload (1 min)
- [ ] On Now Playing, tap **Save** (the heart turns red).
- [ ] Pull down to reload the page, or close the Safari tab and open the site again.
- [ ] The station is under **Favorites** on Home, and **Resume** shows the last station. One tap on either plays it.
- [ ] **Menu → Export favorites** and save the file (Files or AirDrop to your iPad). On the iPad, **Menu → Import favorites from a file**.

## Bonus, in the van
- [ ] Connect via CarPlay or Bluetooth. Audio comes through the van's speakers, and the steering-wheel next/previous buttons change station (unverified; depends on the van).

If anything fails, tell Claude Code which box, what you saw, and whether it was Wi-Fi or cellular.
